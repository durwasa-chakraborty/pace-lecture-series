#### **Talk**
- **Topic:** ocaml-mmtk: Porting OCaml to the MMTk Garbage Collector

- **Description:** ocaml-mmtk is a fork of OCaml where we’ve stripped out the Vanilla GC and instead use the MMTk bindings hooked to the OCaml runtime which handle all allocation and GC tasks.
This enables us to use the GC plans (also called “collectors”) that already ship with MMTk like NoGC (no-op), SemiSpace, MarkSweep, Immix etc.
